Put it on the same calendar as the caterer

A wedding workout plan fails for one reason: it lives in a different app from the wedding. Put your sessions next to the tastings, fittings and family dinners, and you will see which weeks are real and which are fantasy.

One honest rule before you start: this is a plan to feel good and have energy for the day. It is not a countdown to a different body.

Four phases, however long you have

  1. Build the habit. Three small sessions a week. Nothing heroic.
  2. Add one thing. A longer walk, a class, a fourth day.
  3. Hold steady. The busy stretch. Keep the minimum going.
  4. Wedding week. Walks, stretching, sleep. That is the whole programme.

Got months? Spend most of them in phases one and two. Got weeks? Go straight to hold steady. The CDC’s activity guide covers how to build up gradually.

Two people, two goals, one wedding

One of you wants to lift. The other wants to stop getting out of breath on the stairs. Fine. Share the appointment, not the workout. More on that in working out together at different fitness levels.

And make a deal about wedding stress: the walk is a no-logistics zone unless you both agree otherwise.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best workout plan for a wedding?

A repeatable one that fits the wedding calendar: shared activity dates, individual workouts that suit each person, rest, and a backup for busy weeks. Use qualified guidance for a personal training plan.

How long before my wedding should I start working out?

Start with your current routine and calendar whenever you are ready. The useful first step is a session you can repeat, not an aggressive countdown or a promise about how you will look.

What's a good weekly workout split?

Leave space for your existing activities, shared appointments, and rest. Put the wedding tasks on the calendar too; that shows where an actual workout slot can fit.

What is the best workout routine for couples?

One shared slot you both enjoy, plus room for different goals. On the weeks the wedding takes over, three ten-minute challenges each in GymCrush keep the streak alive.

The short answer

If you want Finch energy for fitness, and you have a partner, it is GymCrush. Small daily challenges, cute characters who react to what you do, things to hatch and collect, and a streak. The difference is that you play it as a couple.

What makes Finch work, and what to look for

Finch is a self-care app where you look after a little bird by completing small goals. It works because the goals are tiny, the reward is immediate, and something cute is happy to see you.

For a fitness version, you want the same three things:

  1. Tiny daily tasks, not a 12-week programme.
  2. A character that reacts when you do them, and when you do not.
  3. Things to unlock, so tomorrow is worth opening the app for.

GymCrush: the cute one for couples

Pick an orb. See what hatches.

You start by choosing a mystery orb. Keep showing up, and it hatches into a mascot. There is a whole crew to collect: otters, capybaras, sloths, a penguin with goggles.

Each mascot has moods. They celebrate when you both finish, and look properly worried when the streak is at risk.

  • Three little challenges a day. Squats, a walk, stretching, drinking water. Ten minutes.
  • One streak for the two of you. Here is how the couple streak works.
  • Scenes to unlock for your mascots to live in: a moonlit onsen, an ice cream beach, a lantern garden.
  • Bets and dares. Loser gives the massage. Ideas in these workout bets for couples.
  • A widget with your two mascots on your Home Screen.

One honest difference from Finch: you do not feed or dress a pet. You collect mascots and they react to your day. And it is made for two people. You can start alone, but it comes alive when your partner joins.

Other gamified fitness apps

Finch

The original self-care pet. You can add exercise goals to it, but it is a wellbeing app, not a fitness one, and it does not know your partner exists. Pick GymCrush instead if you want the cute loop built around movement and shared with someone.

Habitica

Turns your habits into a role-playing game with a pixel avatar, quests and gear. Great if you like RPGs and to-do lists. Pick GymCrush instead if you want something softer, cuter and made for a couple.

Walking games

Games like Pikmin Bloom and Walkr reward you for steps. Lovely for walkers. Pick GymCrush instead if you want more than walking to count.

Zombies, Run!

An audio adventure where you run from zombies. Brilliant for runners, not especially cute. Pick GymCrush instead if being chased is not your idea of self-care.

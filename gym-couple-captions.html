Frequently asked questions

What is a good caption for a gym couple photo?

Short ones work best: “My favourite spotter”, “Sweat now, snacks later”, or “Stronger as a pair”. Funny options include “Till deadlift do us part” and “Our couples therapy has dumbbells”.

What are some funny gym couple captions?

Try “He carries the weights, I carry the conversation”, “We only fight over the last squat rack”, or “Relationship status: sharing a protein shaker”.

What is a one-word caption for a gym couple?

Swolemates, Teammates, Together, Unstoppable, or simply Us.

What should I caption a workout photo with my boyfriend or girlfriend?

For him: “The only man allowed to count my reps”. For her: “She out-squats me. I am fine. It is fine.” Or share your streak: “Day 30. Still not broken.”

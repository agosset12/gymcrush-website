Best Couple Workout Apps in 2026: Real iOS Options Compared

“Couple workout app” can mean a timer you share during a circuit, a photo sent after separate workouts, or two gym logs you can both see. Start with the moment you actually want, then compare the apps that do that job.

By Antoine Gosset, founder of GymCrush
Published October 14, 2026 · Last updated October 14, 2026

  • Couple-specific apps you can find on iOS

How this list was built

We searched the US iTunes Search API for “couple workout,” “partner fitness,” “workout with partner,” and “couples fitness,” then checked each included app against its own US App Store listing or first-party website on October 8, 2026. The App Store “Free” label describes downloading the app; it does not tell you which features are free. Where a listing does not make a subscription requirement clear, we say so.

Publisher disclosure: GymCrush publishes this guide and is included below as a coming-soon option. We have given the live apps their own use cases and linked their sources directly. This is a feature-and-fit comparison, not a ranking based on reviews, user counts, or promised results.

For the short version: choose a pair-specific app if you want your relationship to be the center of the experience. Choose a general tracker if you already have an exercise routine you want to share. If all you need is a plan for next Tuesday, the free weekly planner may be enough.

Before you compare screens, pick a few couple workout challenges you actually want to try. You will have a real routine against which to judge each app.

Couple-specific apps you can find on iOS

Sweatmates: Partner Fitness

What it is: a two-person workout accountability app. Its listing describes a weekly workout goal, SweatCam photo logs your partner sees, pair progress, and light wagers such as chores or dinner. Platform: iPhone. Subscription: the listing explicitly says workout logging requires a subscription. Best for: couples who want a weekly photo check-in and a playful wager more than detailed training metrics. Official App Store listing.

FitWithLove: Couples Workout

What it is: a couples workout library. The listing describes tailored routines and partner exercise options including yoga, cardio, and strength. Platform: iPhone. Subscription: the listing shows an in-app purchase for an ad-free experience; it does not clearly state that a recurring subscription is required. Best for: couples who want exercise ideas and guided partner routines. Official App Store listing.

TimeLift Couples Workout Timer

What it is: a timer designed for partner workouts. Its listing describes guided intervals, automatic turns, audio cues, alternating and simultaneous modes, and operation while the phone is locked. Platform: iPhone and iPad. Subscription: TimeLift Pro is offered as an auto-renewing subscription; the listing does not clearly map every feature to the free tier. Best for: a pair doing circuits together who wants the turn-taking handled. Official App Store listing.

DuoDone: Couple Workout Proof

What it is: a one-to-one workout promise and check-in app. The listing describes choosing workout days and a deadline, recording completion with a photo or a “Did it” tap, rest passes, partner acknowledgment, a shared streak, and non-money stakes. Platform: iPhone. Subscription: the current listing does not clearly specify a required subscription; verify access in the app. Best for: couples who want a specific promise-and-proof ritual. Official App Store listing.

GymBuddy75: Fitness Together

What it is: a paired fitness challenge with personal plans. The listing describes GymBuddy and SupportBuddy modes, workouts, nutrition targets, photo check-ins, daily progress, and a strike system. A pair can be partners, spouses, or friends. Platform: iPhone and iPad. Subscription: the listing does not clearly explain ongoing paid access; check in the app. Best for: a pair who wants a structured challenge with separate plans and food logging. Official App Store listing.

General workout apps couples also use

These products are broader fitness tools. Their value for a couple depends on the sport or kind of sharing you already enjoy.

Hevy

What it is: a gym workout tracker. Official sources describe routine building, workout logs, progress tracking, social following, and sharing workouts or routines. Platforms: iOS and Android; Apple Watch and Wear OS are described on its site. Subscription: a Pro subscription is offered, while the app also has free access. Best for: two people who already lift and want to log their own sessions and see each other’s training. Official App Store listing.

Strava

What it is: an activity tracker and social feed for sports including runs, rides, and walks. Its official site describes activity sharing and community features; its support site explains group challenges. Platforms: iPhone and Apple Watch are listed on the App Store; the official site also offers Android. Subscription: a subscription offers additional features, while basic use is available without one. Best for: couples who already record outdoor activities and like following each other’s sessions. Official App Store listing.

Apple Fitness+

What it is: Apple’s guided workout and meditation service. Apple describes workout categories, Custom Plans, recommendations, and Family Sharing. Platforms: iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, and Apple Watch integration, subject to Apple’s device requirements. Subscription: Fitness+ is a subscription service. Best for: a couple who wants a class menu and a planned session on Apple devices. Official Apple Fitness+ page.

Choose by the moment you want to share

If the workout itself needs coordination, look at TimeLift. If the fun part is a photo after independent sessions, compare Sweatmates and DuoDone. If you want a couple-oriented exercise library, look at FitWithLove; for a longer structured pair challenge, read GymBuddy75’s current description. If you already lift, run, or love guided classes, the general apps may fit naturally into a routine you already have.

Check both phones, your region, and each listing’s current payment terms before choosing. A subscription label can change, and a free download is not a promise that the feature you want is free. You can also use a calendar and shared note to see whether a check-in ritual is worth keeping.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best app for couples to work out together?

It depends on what “together” means. TimeLift helps with live partner timing; Sweatmates and DuoDone focus on check-ins; FitWithLove offers couple routines. Hevy, Strava, and Fitness+ serve couples with existing gym, outdoor, or class habits.

What is the best workout program for couples?

An app can provide a class, routine, challenge, or timer, but the useful starting program is one both of you can actually put on the calendar. Choose separate versions when your fitness levels differ.

What apps can couples use together?

Couple-specific iOS options include Sweatmates, FitWithLove, TimeLift, DuoDone, and GymBuddy75. Couples also use general tools such as Hevy, Strava, and Apple Fitness+.

What is a good accountability workout partner app?

For a direct pair ritual, choose whether you want a photo, a promise and shared streak, or a timed session. Review the official listing’s payment terms before committing.

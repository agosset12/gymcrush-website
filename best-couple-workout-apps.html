The winner: GymCrush

GymCrush is the best workout app for couples. It is the only one here that turns “we should work out more” into a game you play together, every day.

  • Three little challenges each, daily. A walk, a plank, ten squats. Done in ten minutes, no gym needed.
  • One streak for the two of you. It only grows when you both finish. See how the couple streak works.
  • Bad days are covered. Team Assist lets one of you carry the other. A Streak Freeze saves the day you both forget.
  • Loser pays. Put a massage or dinner duty on the line. Here are 25 workout bets for couples.
  • Things hatch. Pick a mystery orb, keep the streak alive, and collect a whole crew of mascots.
  • Your partner on your Home Screen. Their latest photo and your two mascots, right in a widget.
  • One subscription covers you both.

What makes a couples app worth keeping

Every app on this list will track something. Very few give you a reason to come back tomorrow. These are the five things we looked for:

  1. Is it daily? Weekly goals get pushed to Sunday.
  2. Is it shared? One streak for two people beats two streaks side by side.
  3. Does it forgive a bad day? If one cold kills the streak, you will both quit.
  4. Does it work at different levels? You should not have to match each other.
  5. Is it fun? Honestly the one that matters most.

GymCrush is the only app here that ticks all five. If you want to test the idea first, try a week of these couple workout challenges.

The other couple apps

Sweatmates

A weekly workout goal for two. You log each workout with a photo your partner sees, and owe a small wager if you miss the week.

Good for: couples who only want a weekly photo check-in. Pick GymCrush instead if: you want something to do every day, a streak you can save on a bad day, and a game around it. Full breakdown in our Sweatmates alternative guide. App Store listing.

DuoDone

A promise-and-proof app. You pick workout days and a deadline, then confirm with a photo or a tap.

Good for: a simple “did you do it” ritual. Pick GymCrush instead if: you want daily challenges to choose from, rewards, and something cute waiting when you finish. App Store listing.

TimeLift

An interval timer for partner circuits, with turn-taking and audio cues.

Good for: timing a circuit you are doing side by side. Pick GymCrush instead if: you are not always in the same room, or you want a habit and not only a stopwatch. App Store listing.

FitWithLove

A library of partner exercises: yoga, cardio, and strength routines to do together.

Good for: ideas for a session you do in the same place. Pick GymCrush instead if: your schedules do not line up, or you want a reason to show up tomorrow as well. App Store listing.

GymBuddy75

A strict paired challenge with workouts, nutrition targets, photo check-ins, and strikes.

Good for: two people who want a hard, rules-heavy programme. Pick GymCrush instead if: you want something light enough to keep for months, not weeks. App Store listing.

Big fitness apps couples borrow

These are great at what they do. None of them was made for two people.

Strava

A tracker and social feed for runs, rides, and walks.

Good for: couples who both already run or ride. Pick GymCrush instead if: one of you does not, or you want it to be about the two of you and not a feed. App Store listing.

Hevy

A gym log for sets, reps, and routines, with a way to follow other lifters.

Good for: two people who both already lift. Pick GymCrush instead if: one of you is not a gym person, or logging sets is not your idea of romance. App Store listing.

Apple Fitness+

Apple’s library of guided workout and meditation classes.

Good for: following a class on the TV together. Pick GymCrush instead if: you want accountability between classes, a shared streak, or anything that knows you are a couple. Apple Fitness+ page.

Already love one of these? Keep it for logging, and use GymCrush for the part that is about the two of you.

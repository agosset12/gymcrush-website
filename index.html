The gamified fitness app for couples. Train together, keep one shared streak, and unlock collectible companions as you go.

How it works

Three steps, then it is just the two of you showing up every day.

1

Pair with your partner

Send them an invite link or a 6-character code. One tap and your two accounts are linked.

2

Finish your daily tasks

Pick challenges that fit your day. Your progress and theirs both count toward the same goal.

3

Keep the streak alive

Hit the goal together and your shared streak ticks up. Miss it and you both feel it.

Built for two

Every mechanic in GymCrush assumes there are two of you. That is the whole point.

🔥

One shared streak

Not one streak each — one between you. It survives only if you both show up, which is exactly the pressure that works.

🤝

Team Assist

Both daily goals merge into a single bar. When one of you has a brutal day, the other can carry it.

🛡️

Streak Freeze

Life happens. Arm a freeze before a day you know is lost and your streak survives it intact.

🥚

Collectible companions

Earn coins together and hatch them into a shared collection of characters. Equip your favourite; they will see it.

📸

Moments & widget

Send a photo straight to your partner's home screen. Private to the two of you, and entirely optional.

💞

One subscription, both of you

Premium is priced per couple, not per person. If one of you subscribes, you both get it.

Where we stand on your data

A couples app holds intimate things. Here is our position, in plain terms.

🚫

No ads, ever

No advertising trackers, no IDFA, no App Tracking Transparency prompt — because we do not track you across other apps.

🔒

Your photos stay yours

Moments are readable only by you and your partner, enforced on the server. We never scan them or train models on them.

🍏

No Apple Health access

We never connect to Apple Health. The only activity data we hold is what you tell the app yourself.

One number. Both of you.

Every day, each of you picks three little challenges. A walk. A plank. Ten squats while the kettle boils. When you have both finished, the streak goes up by one and your mascots throw a tiny party.

It is not two streaks side by side. There is one, and it belongs to both of you. That changes how skipping feels. Not sure what your three should be? Start with these couple workout challenges.

What happens at 9pm

Your mascots start to panic

If the day is not done by evening, your mascots stop smiling. They look worried on your Home Screen, on your widget, and on your partner’s phone too.

A countdown appears on your Lock Screen. Nothing dramatic. Just two small animals who believed in you.

Three ways to save a bad day

  • Team Assist. Your goals merge into one bar of six. If you can only manage one, your partner does five. That is love.
  • Streak Freeze. A shield for the day you both forget. Arm it and a missed day does not send you back to zero.
  • Dare Drop. When it gets too comfortable, send a dare and see if they blink.

Most streak apps punish you for being human. This one assumes you have a job, a cold, and a partner who has your back.

What the streak unlocks

Every finished day moves you along a reward path that runs for 90 days: coins, mystery pouches, new scenes, and orbs.

And then there are the orbs

Pick one. You do not know what is inside. Keep the streak alive and it hatches into a mascot you keep.

There is a whole crew to collect. You will say you do not care. You will have a favourite within a week.

Put something on it

Loser buys the coffee

Add a stake to the streak: a massage, dinner duty, breakfast in bed. Suddenly “I will go tomorrow” is a much harder sell.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a streak app for couples?

Yes. GymCrush is a couple streak app: you and your partner share one daily streak that grows when you both finish three small challenges.

What is the best couple streak app?

GymCrush. It is built around one shared streak, with Team Assist so one partner can carry the other, a Streak Freeze to protect a missed day, and mascots and rewards that unlock as the streak grows.

What happens to a couple streak if one person misses a day?

In GymCrush, Team Assist lets the other partner complete more challenges to cover, and a Streak Freeze protects the streak on a day you both miss.

Can long-distance couples keep a streak together?

Yes. Each partner completes their own challenges wherever they are, and the shared streak, notifications, photos, and widget keep you connected.

Gifts for a Gym Boyfriend or Girlfriend: 30 Ideas They Will Actually Use

Your person loves the gym. You love your person. You have no idea what a “lifting strap” is and the internet keeps suggesting a 20-kilo kettlebell, which is a hard thing to wrap. Here are 30 ideas, sorted by how much effort they take.

By Antoine Gosset, founder of GymCrush

Small gifts under a tenner or so

  • Good gym socks. Unglamorous. Always welcome.
  • A shaker bottle that does not leak in the bag.
  • Lifting straps or wrist wraps. Ask a gym friend which they use.
  • Resistance bands. For warm-ups and hotel rooms.
  • A gym towel that is not the one from the bathroom.
  • Lip balm and a small deodorant for the bag. Practical is romantic.
  • A skipping rope.
  • Their favourite protein bar, in bulk.

Recovery gifts

  • A massage gun. The gift every gym person wants and will not buy themselves.
  • A foam roller. They will complain and use it daily.
  • Bath salts and an evening off.
  • A proper massage, booked, with the date already picked.
  • A sleep mask and a decent pillow. Recovery is mostly sleep.
  • A heat pack for sore shoulders.

Things they will wear or carry

  • A gym bag with a shoe compartment.
  • A big water bottle with a straw.
  • Headphones that survive sweat.
  • A matching set. Check the size on something they already own.
  • A hoodie they will steal back from you.
  • Lifting shoes, only if you know the exact model they want.

Experiences

  • A class pass to somewhere new: climbing, boxing, pilates.
  • A session with a coach for the lift they are stuck on.
  • An entry to an event: a 5K, a hike weekend, a fun race.
  • A day pass to a fancy gym or spa.
  • A meal-prep afternoon. You cook, they pick the menu.

  2. A playlist made for their next heavy day.
  3. A framed photo of the two of you after a workout, looking terrible and happy.

Frequently asked questions

What do you get a boyfriend who loves the gym?

Things he will use every session: lifting straps, a shaker that does not leak, a massage gun, or a class pass. For a gift you share, set up GymCrush so you train together every day.

What is a good gift for a gym girlfriend?

A matching set in her size, a massage, sweat-proof headphones, or a class she has wanted to try. A shared streak in GymCrush is a gift for both of you.

What is a good Christmas gift for a fitness couple?

Something you both use: a class pass for two, a spa day, or GymCrush, where one subscription covers both partners and you keep a streak together.

What are cheap gifts for a gym lover?

Gym socks, resistance bands, a skipping rope, a shaker bottle, or a handmade coupon book for massages and spots.

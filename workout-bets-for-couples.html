Workout Bets for Couples: 25 Loser-Pays Ideas

Nothing gets a person off the couch like the risk of doing the dishes for a week. A good workout bet is small, a bit silly, and paid in time or effort, never in guilt. Here are 25 to steal, and the rules that keep them fun.

By Antoine Gosset, founder of GymCrush
Published November 13, 2026 · Last updated November 16, 2026

Rules for a bet that stays fun

  • Bet on showing up, not on results. “Who misses a day first” works. “Who loses more weight” does not.
  • Make it fair for two different levels. Each person sets their own target. See working out at different fitness levels.
  • Keep the stake small and kind. If losing would start a fight, the stake is too big.
  • Set an end date. One week is plenty for a first bet.
  • Pay up fast. A bet that is never settled kills the next one.

Bets where the loser serves

  • Loser cooks dinner. Winner picks the menu.
  • Loser gives a 10-minute massage.
  • Loser does the dishes for a week.
  • Loser makes coffee every morning for five days.
  • Loser does the next laundry run alone.
  • Loser plans and pays for date night.
  • Loser carries the gym bags for a month.
  • Loser brings breakfast in bed on Sunday.

Bets where the winner chooses

  • Winner picks the next film, no veto.
  • Winner controls the playlist in the car for a week.
  • Winner picks the next holiday activity.
  • Winner chooses the next workout you do together.
  • Winner gets the good side of the sofa for a week.
  • Winner picks the restaurant for the next three dates.
  • Winner chooses the loser’s gym outfit for one session.
  • Winner gets one “get out of chores” card.

Silly bets

  • Loser posts a flattering photo of the winner with a caption the winner writes.
  • Loser does the winner’s warm-up as a dramatic interpretive dance.
  • Loser has to say “you were right” on video.
  • Loser wears the ugliest socks in the house to the gym.
  • Loser orders dessert and gives the winner the first bite.
  • Loser learns and performs a 15-second dance.
  • Loser narrates the winner’s next workout like a sports commentator.
  • Loser writes a three-line poem about the winner’s biceps.
  • Loser changes their phone wallpaper to the winner’s gym selfie for a week.

Frequently asked questions

What are good workout bets for couples?

Small loser-pays stakes work best: loser cooks dinner, gives a massage, does the dishes for a week, or plans date night. Bet on showing up, not on results.

What should the loser of a fitness bet do?

Something small, kind, and quick to pay: a chore, a meal, a massage, or letting the winner choose the next date.

How do you make a fitness bet fair between partners?

Let each person set their own target so different fitness levels do not matter, bet on consistency instead of performance, and set a clear end date.

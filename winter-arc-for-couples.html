What a winter arc actually is

The winter arc is a TikTok trend that took off in autumn 2024. The idea: use the last months of the year to quietly build your habits, so you walk into January already moving while everyone else is writing resolutions.

It usually starts on October 1 and runs to the end of the year. There are no official rules. You write your own, often as a little “contract”. Starting late is completely fine. A winter arc that begins in late November still beats a resolution that begins in January.

Why it works better with two

  • Winter is when motivation dies. It is dark at 5pm and the sofa is warm. A partner who is also doing it is the difference between going and not going.
  • The solo version is a bit grim. “No distractions, no excuses” is a lot. “We both did our three, now we are watching a film” is sustainable.
  • You already share the hard part. Same kitchen, same evenings, same temptations.

Not at the same level? That is fine. See working out together at different fitness levels.

Winter arc rules for couples

Write your own contract. Here is one to steal and edit.

  1. Three small things a day, each. A walk, a workout, a stretch. Your three do not have to match.
  2. Tell each other when it is done. That is the whole accountability system.
  3. One thing together every week. A long walk, a class, a gym date.
  4. Phones down for the first hour of the evening.
  5. Lights out at a time you agree on, at least on weeknights.
  6. A missed day is a missed day. No restarting from zero. You carry on tomorrow.
  7. Loser pays. Whoever skips first owes the other one something.

Frequently asked questions

What is a winter arc for couples?

A winter arc for couples is the TikTok winter arc trend done as a pair: you both commit to small daily habits for the last months of the year, keep each other accountable, and head into January already in a routine.

When does the winter arc start and end?

It usually starts on October 1 and runs to the end of the year, about 90 days. There are no official rules, and starting late is fine.

What are the winter arc rules?

There are no official rules. Most people write their own contract. For couples, a good set is three small daily challenges each, one activity together every week, and telling each other when the day is done.

Is there a winter arc app for couples?

Yes. GymCrush works as a winter arc tracker for two: each partner gets three small daily challenges, you share one streak, and Team Assist and a Streak Freeze keep it alive on bad days.

Can you start a winter arc in November or December?

Yes. The point is to build habits before January, so any start before New Year counts.

Long-Distance Couple Apps: 7 That Make the Miles Feel Shorter

You have a group chat with exactly two people in it, a shared playlist, and a countdown to the next flight. You do not need another app to talk. You need something to actually do together. Here are seven, starting with that one.

The short answer

Most long-distance apps help you talk. Very few give you something to do. Our pick is GymCrush, because it gives you a small thing to finish together every single day, from two different cities.

Then add one app for watching things together and one for seeing each other’s day. That is the whole stack. You do not need seven.

1. GymCrush: do something together every day

Each of you picks three little challenges a day: a walk, a stretch, ten squats. You do yours in your morning, they do theirs in their evening, and one streak belongs to both of you.

  • Their Lock Screen lights up the moment you finish, whatever the time difference.
  • A widget with their latest photo and your two mascots sits on your Home Screen.
  • Pokes. Tap their mascot to see their day. If you both poke, you might hit the coin jackpot.
  • Bets for the next visit. Loser plans the first date. Ideas: workout bets for couples.
  • Things to hatch together. Mascots, scenes, a 90-day reward path.

It turns “how was your day?” into something you both did. How to set it up across time zones: long-distance couple workouts.

2 and 3. Watch something together

Teleparty

Syncs a film or series so you both press play at the same moment, with a chat on the side. The classic long-distance date night.

Rave

Another way to watch in sync, from your phones. Good when one of you is travelling.

Use these for: Friday night. They will not help with: the other six days.

4. See each other’s day

Locket Widget

Photos from your partner appear in a widget on your Home Screen. Simple and sweet.

Pick GymCrush instead if you want the photo widget and a reason to send the photo: a finished challenge, a streak day, a dare.

5 and 6. Talk about more than logistics

Paired

A daily question for the two of you, plus quizzes and games. Good for the conversations you keep meaning to have.

Marco Polo

Video messages you watch when you are free. Useful when your time zones never overlap.

7. Plan the next visit

Cupla

A shared calendar built for couples, so the next visit, the flights and the date nights are all in one place.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best app for long-distance couples?

GymCrush is our pick, because it gives you something to do together every day: three small challenges each, one shared streak, photos on each other’s Lock Screen, and a widget with your partner on your Home Screen.

What apps do long-distance couples use?

GymCrush for a shared daily habit, Teleparty or Rave for watching films together, Locket Widget for photos, Paired for daily questions, Marco Polo for video messages, and a shared calendar such as Cupla.

Is there a long-distance couple app with a widget?

Yes. GymCrush has Home Screen widgets that show your partner’s latest photo or your two mascots, and Locket Widget shows photos from your partner.

Are there long-distance couple apps with games?

GymCrush is a game for two: you keep a streak, collect mascots, place bets and send dares. Paired also includes quizzes and games.

What can long-distance couples do together every day?

Pick three small challenges each, do them in your own time zone, and keep one streak together. In GymCrush your partner is notified the moment you finish.

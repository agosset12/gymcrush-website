Fitness Goals for Couples: Pick Goals You Both Want to Keep

“Get fit together” sounds great on a Sunday walk. By Thursday, it is unclear whether you meant a gym class, a healthier dinner, or finally using those shoes by the door. Give the two of you a goal you can put in the calendar.

By Antoine Gosset, founder of GymCrush

Pick a doing-goal, not a looking-goal

“Get fit together” is a vibe, not a goal. “Lose 5 kilos” is a goal, but it is about one body and nobody can do anything about it on a Tuesday.

A good couple goal is something you do: a walk every evening, a class on Saturdays, ten minutes each before breakfast. You can tick it off. You can do it badly and it still counts.

The goal menu

  • Move for ten minutes a day, each.
  • One active date every weekend.
  • Try one new activity a month. Rate it afterwards.
  • Walk 8,000 steps, three days a week.
  • Stretch before bed, together.
  • Hold a two-minute plank by spring.
  • Run your first 5K, at your own pace, on the same day.
  • Cook one new recipe together every week.
  • Drink water before coffee. Yes, it counts.
  • Keep one shared streak going for 30 days.

Make it survive a bad week

The first missed day is where most goals quietly die. Decide now what a miss means: nothing. You carry on tomorrow.

This is the part GymCrush is built for. Each of you gets three small challenges a day. If one of you is wiped out, Team Assist lets the other cover. If you both forget, a Streak Freeze has your back.

Check in on Sundays

Five minutes. Three questions.

  1. What did we actually do?
  2. What did we dread?
  3. What are we changing?

Swap out anything either of you dreaded. A goal you hate is not a goal, it is a chore with branding.

Let the goal hatch something

Goals stick when something happens every time you show up. In GymCrush each finished day moves you along a 90-day reward path, with coins, scenes and mascots to unlock. It works as a habit tracker for couples too.

Frequently asked questions

What are some good couple's goals?

Choose a shared action you both want: a weekly active date, trying a class, a walking route, or sending a finish check-in. Keep individual training goals separate.

What are 5 fitness goals?

For a couple, try a booked activity date, a shared walking route, separate workouts with a common finish, a beginner class together, and a weekly plan review. Make each one specific to your own schedule.

What are some good fitness ideas for couples?

Walking dates, home bodyweight sessions, guided classes, gym visits with separate routines, and long-distance activity relays are all options. Choose one you both want to repeat.

What is the best workout routine for couples?

One with a shared goal and room for each person’s own activity. Book the time, agree on the check-in, and track it with one shared streak in GymCrush so the goal stays visible to both of you.

Sweatmates Alternative: The Couples App That Feels Like a Game

Sweatmates gives you a weekly goal and a photo. GymCrush gives you a little world you build with your person: a streak you protect together, bets where the loser owes a massage, and a mystery orb that only hatches when you both show up. Guess which one you will still be opening in March.

The 10-second version

Sweatmates is a check-in. GymCrush is a game for two. Same goal, keep each other moving. Very different feeling when you open it.

GymCrush publishes this page. What we say about Sweatmates comes from its own App Store page, checked on November 13, 2026.

What a day in GymCrush looks like

Pick your three

Every day you each choose three little challenges from a whole wall of them. Squats. A two-minute plank. A walk. Stretching before bed. Ten minutes and you are done.

No gym needed, and your three do not have to match theirs. Need ideas? Steal from these couple workout challenges.

One streak. Both of you.

When you both finish, the streak goes up and your mascots throw a tiny party. Skip, and they start looking very worried around 9pm.

It is one number that belongs to the two of you. Nobody wants to be the one who broke day 36.

Make a bet. Loser pays.

Massage. Dinner duty. Breakfast in bed. Put something on the streak and suddenly “I will go tomorrow” gets a lot less convincing.

Snap the proof, mark it settled, gloat responsibly. Here are 25 workout bets for couples to start with.

Bad day? Your person has your back.

Team Assist merges your goals into one bar of six, so one of you can carry the other. A Streak Freeze saves the day you both forget.

And when things get too comfortable, drop a dare.

Hatch something cute

Pick a mystery orb. Keep showing up. Watch it hatch. There is a whole crew of mascots to collect, scenes to unlock, and a reward path that runs for 90 days.

This is the part people do not expect to care about. Then they have a favourite.

Send the proof, if you feel like it

Photos are optional. When you do send one, it lands on your partner’s Lock Screen and on the widget on their Home Screen.

Or just poke them. If you both poke at the same time, you might hit the coin jackpot.

Why couples switch

  • Weekly goals get pushed to Sunday. Daily ones get done before dinner.
  • Not everyone wants to take a gym selfie every time. In GymCrush you finish with a tap.
  • You are not at the same level, and that is fine. Each of you picks your own three. More on working out at different fitness levels.
  • You are not always in the same city. The streak, the photos, and the widget travel. See long-distance couple workouts.
  • It is actually fun to open. That is the whole trick.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best Sweatmates alternative?

GymCrush. It turns working out with your partner into a game: three little daily challenges each, one shared streak, loser-pays bets, dares, and mascots you hatch and collect together.

What is the difference between Sweatmates and GymCrush?

Sweatmates is a weekly workout goal logged with photos. GymCrush is a daily game for two: three small challenges each, one shared streak with Team Assist and a Streak Freeze, bets, dares, and a collection of mascots. Photos are optional.

Is GymCrush better than Sweatmates for couples?

Yes. GymCrush gives you something small to do together every day, protects your streak on bad days, and is genuinely fun to open, which is what keeps both of you coming back.

Do we have to go to the gym to use GymCrush?

No. Challenges include walks, stretching, squats, planks, and yoga, so you can finish your three at home in about ten minutes.

Does GymCrush work for long-distance couples?

Yes. Each of you completes your own challenges wherever you are, and the shared streak, photos, pokes, and Home Screen widget keep you connected.

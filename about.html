About GymCrush

Who makes GymCrush?

GymCrush is made by Antoine Gosset, its solo founder. Antoine publishes the app and this website as a sole trader. You can find the publisher details in the legal notice.

Why a fitness app for couples?

GymCrush is an iOS fitness app for couples with shared daily challenges, one shared streak, and workout photos for each other.

The idea is a daily ritual you can share, even when your schedules are different: the same challenges, one streak, and a notification when your partner shows up. Stakes, dares, and moments give you ways to make that routine your own.

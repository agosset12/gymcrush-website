75 Hard for Couples: The Rules, and a Version You Will Actually Finish

Day 1: you are both up at 6am, water bottles filled, feeling unstoppable. Day 9: one of you had a glass of wine at a birthday and now, by the rules, you are both back at day 1. Here is how 75 Hard works, and how to do 75 days together without that happening.

The 75 Hard rules

75 Hard was created by Andy Frisella as a mental-toughness programme. For 75 days in a row you:

  1. Follow a diet of your choice, with no alcohol and no cheat meals.
  2. Do two 45-minute workouts a day. One must be outdoors.
  3. Drink a gallon of water.
  4. Read ten pages of a non-fiction book.
  5. Take a progress photo.

Miss one item on any day and you start again from day 1. It is meant to be extreme. Doctors point out it is not for everyone; the Cleveland Clinic’s overview is worth a read before you start.

Why couples quit it

  • The restart rule punishes both of you. One slip, and somebody has to decide whether you both go back to zero.
  • Two workouts a day is three hours of your evening, every evening, for two and a half months.
  • No dinners out, no drinks, no dessert. For 75 days. With the person you date.
  • You are probably not at the same level. The rules are identical for both of you anyway.

75 Soft: the gentler version

75 Soft keeps the 75 days and drops the punishment:

  1. Eat well, and only drink on social occasions.
  2. Train for 45 minutes a day, with one day a week of active recovery.
  3. Drink three litres of water.
  4. Read ten pages of anything.

No restart if you miss a day. For most couples this is already a much better fit.

75 Together: the couples version

This one is ours. It keeps what works, the 75 days and the daily tick, and adds the thing the other two are missing: each other.

  1. Three small challenges a day, each. Pick your own. A walk, squats, a stretch, water.
  2. One shared streak. It goes up when you have both finished.
  3. One active date a week.
  4. No restart. If one of you is ill, the other covers. If you both miss, you use a freeze.
  5. A bet on day 75. Decide the prize on day 1.

Frequently asked questions

What is the 75 Hard couples challenge?

It is 75 Hard done as a pair: both partners follow the five daily rules for 75 days. Many couples switch to a gentler version, because under the original rules one missed task sends you back to day 1.

What are the 75 Hard rules?

Follow a diet with no alcohol or cheat meals, do two 45-minute workouts a day with one outdoors, drink a gallon of water, read ten pages of non-fiction, and take a progress photo, every day for 75 days. Missing anything means starting over.

What is the 75 Soft challenge for couples?

75 Soft is the gentler version: eat well and only drink on social occasions, train 45 minutes a day with one recovery day a week, drink three litres of water, and read ten pages. There is no restart rule, which makes it easier to do together.

Is there an app for a 75-day couples challenge?

Yes. GymCrush gives each partner three small daily challenges and one shared streak, with Team Assist and a Streak Freeze so a single bad day does not end the challenge.

Can couples do 75 Hard at different fitness levels?

The original rules are the same for everyone. A couples version works better when each partner picks their own daily challenges and you share the streak instead of the workout.

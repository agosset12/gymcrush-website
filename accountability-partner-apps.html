Why your partner beats a stranger

They already care

A coach you pay or a stranger from a forum will forget you by Friday. Your partner notices when you skip, and is genuinely happy when you do not.

The only problem is that “did you work out today?” sounds like nagging when a person says it. It sounds very different when a small cartoon raccoon says it.

What a good accountability app has to do

  • Make it visible. Your partner should know you finished without you having to announce it.
  • Keep it small. Daily and tiny beats weekly and heroic.
  • Forgive a bad day. If one miss ends everything, you will both stop.
  • Give skipping a playful cost. A stake, a dare, a streak you do not want to break.
  • Be fun. You are supposed to want to open it.

Accountability without the nagging

  1. Cheer, do not police. “Nice, you did it” lands better than “did you do it?”
  2. Agree what a miss means before it happens. Tomorrow is a new day and the streak has a freeze.
  3. Keep the stake small and silly. If losing would start a fight, it is too big.

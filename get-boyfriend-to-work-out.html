How to Get Your Boyfriend to Work Out (Without Nagging)

It is 9pm. You have shoes on. He is on the couch saying “later,” which, in couch language, could mean next spring. You want a workout buddy and more time together. Start with the second thing.

Lead with the date, not the diagnosis

“I miss doing stuff with you. Walk after dinner tomorrow?” is an invitation he can picture. “You really need to exercise” is a performance review at the front door.

Give him a real choice and a real time. Tuesday after dinner beats “we should work out sometime.” If he picks the walk, book the walk. Do not quietly upgrade it to hill sprints.

What “later” actually means

Ask once: “Is it the timing, the gym, or just not your thing?” Then believe the answer.

  • “I hate gyms.” Fine. A walk to a café, a beginner dance class, a living-room playlist. The gym is a place, not the point.
  • “You are way fitter than me.” Share the start and the finish, do your own thing in between.
  • “I do not know what to do.” Pick something simple he already knows, or a class where you are both new.
  • “I never have time.” One small slot. Ten minutes counts.
  • “I will start Monday.” Great. Which Monday, and doing what?

If keeping up is the worry, read how to work out together at different fitness levels. Nobody should have to audition.

First dates he will actually say yes to

  • A walk with a destination. Bakery, viewpoint, the good coffee place.
  • Something neither of you is good at. Bouldering, badminton, a dance class. Being bad together is bonding.
  • His thing. If he likes football, go kick a ball. You wanted time together, remember.
  • Ten minutes at home. No audience, his playlist.

The one-week test

  1. Monday: ask what he would enjoy. Book one thing.
  2. Wednesday: ten-minute walk after dinner. Phones away.
  3. Saturday: the thing you booked. Then lunch.
  4. Sunday: one question. “Would you do that again?”

If yes, put next week in the weekly couple planner. If no, try a different activity before you decide he is a lost cause.

Let a raccoon do the nagging

It lands differently from a mascot

“Did you work out today?” sounds like nagging when you say it. It sounds completely different when a small cartoon animal looks worried on his Home Screen.

In GymCrush he picks his own three little challenges, you pick yours, and you keep one streak together. He never has to match your workout. He just has to not be the one who broke day 12.

And if he still says no after a fair try? Keep your own routine, and plan a different kind of date. You cannot drag someone into a hobby.

Frequently asked questions

What can I do if my partner doesn't want to workout?

Invite him to a specific activity once, ask what puts him off, and accept his answer. Keep your own routine and plan another kind of date if what you really want is time together.

How to motivate your boyfriend at the gym?

Let him choose the activity or part of the visit, arrive with separate plans, and ask what kind of encouragement he likes. A shared start and finish can feel more relaxed than following every set together.

What is the best workout routine for couples?

One you would both repeat: a booked slot, activities you each like, and a quick check-in afterward. Many couples start with three small daily challenges each and one shared streak in GymCrush, so he never has to match your workout.

What are some good fitness ideas for couples?

Try a neighborhood walk, a beginner class, a gym visit with separate routines, a two-song dance date, or a shared activity at different times followed by a photo check-in.
